Health Insurance Risk Calculator

Estimate your health insurance risk category in under a minute.

How it works

Enter your age, height, weight, blood pressure, and family history. The calculator works out your BMI and blood pressure category, then adds up risk points for each factor. The total puts you in one of these categories: low risk, moderate risk, high risk, or uninsurable.

This is an estimate for a class project, not medical or insurance advice.
